Joint customer selection and route construction are tightly coupled in team-routing problems, yet prior methods weakly connect these decisions. We propose coupled neighborhood search, which combines a unified solution representation with a coupled insertion neighborhood to modify selection and routing jointly. On the verified benchmark set, the method improves the average objective value by 2.4\% relative to Baseline A. The results show that directly coordinating selection and sequencing can improve solution quality for the deterministic instances considered.
