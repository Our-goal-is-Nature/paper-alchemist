## Extraction caveat

Automatic section counts are routing diagnostics, not verified prose counts. Mixed columns and section spillover were manually reviewed; do not impose the seed statistics as style quotas.

## Rhetorical moves

Lead with an unresolved measurement or representation issue, specify the comparison and sample, give the central directional findings, and close with a bounded practical implication. This sequence is visible in the website-metrics, hyperparameter and occupational-usability abstracts.

## Organization

Keep context subordinate to the study. Separate method, findings and implications even within one paragraph. The usability abstract gives group sizes before contrasts; the hyperparameter abstract names the manipulated setting before demographic deviations.

## Language and stance

Use direct study-action verbs for performed work and qualified language for consequences. Distinguish observed differences from suggested design implications. Do not reproduce author-specific phrases.

## Evidence and citations

Use only manuscript-confirmed sample sizes, metrics and results. The website abstract reports directional percentages; the qualitative abstract combines thematic contrasts with moderator speech proportions. No universal citation or length quota is supported.

## Cross-lingual transferable patterns

Transfer problem–study–finding–implication order to Chinese, not English syntax. There is no Chinese corpus evidence.

## Do

Name the comparison, unit of analysis and strongest relevant finding; preserve denominators and qualifiers during polishing.

## Avoid

Do not infer abstract conventions from the ESWA packet, which contains methods, or the reflection packet, which contains closing remarks. Exclude adjacent-column introduction and publisher metadata.

## Generation checklist

Check that every factual clause occurs in the supplied manuscript; confirm the abstract contains an actual result rather than only promises; do not add missing numbers.
