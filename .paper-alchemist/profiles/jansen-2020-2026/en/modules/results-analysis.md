## Extraction caveat

## Rhetorical moves

Present descriptive context before question-specific comparisons; state the direction or theme, point to the relevant table or figure, then interpret its meaning within scope. Separate interpretation and practical implications from observations.

## Organization

Use RQ or hypothesis labels where the manuscript has them. Website metrics distinguish absolute measurement disagreement from correlated rankings; longitudinal analysis distinguishes persona persistence from topical consistency. Qualitative analysis compares groups through coded categories and participant evidence.

## Language and stance

Use precise result language and calibrated interpretations. Association, variation and model accuracy do not justify causal claims. Qualitative contrasts require careful scope rather than statistical embellishment.

## Evidence and citations

Introduce visuals with their comparison purpose and interpret a selected pattern, not every cell. Preserve sample sizes, denominators, test statistics and supplied quotes exactly. The website study illustrates test–direction–hypothesis decision and correlation–interpretation sequences.

## Cross-lingual transferable patterns

Transfer evidence–contrast–interpretation logic while retaining the manuscript language and citation style. No Chinese surface convention is established.

## Do

Distinguish measured counts from proxies; report uncertainty and deviations honestly. For conceptual system papers, treat diagrams and use cases as demonstrations, not controlled empirical validation.

## Avoid

Four sources are visual-supplement routed and some packets contain opening pages rather than results. Their presence does not establish nine independent prose exemplars. Do not infer unlabeled values from figures or turn mixed-column table text into facts.

## Generation checklist

Does every comparison have a stated reference and denominator? Are claims supported by supplied evidence? Are implications and limitations separated from numerical outcomes?
