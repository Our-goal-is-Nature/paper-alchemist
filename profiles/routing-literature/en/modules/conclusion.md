## Rhetorical moves

- Close the motivating gap with supported answers and bounded consequences, then specific next verification. Early IR papers emphasize future user/system research; log/campaign papers explain stakeholder utility; short learning papers explicitly remain exploratory; conceptual/reflection work requires a different closure.

## Organization

- Brief summary or future-oriented close varies by venue; some papers have no standalone conclusion and the 2018 study uses an actual combined Discussion and Conclusion. Absence must remain absence, not completed invented prose.

## Language and stance

- Use may/could/appears for future design and external transfer; strong historical assertions do not override local limitations. Same author does not imply one universal stance.

## Evidence and citations

- Restate established evidence without new results. A proposed standard session threshold, profitable ad strategy or manageable persona set is task/data-dependent; past platform attributes are not contemporary findings.

## Cross-lingual transferable patterns

- Transfer supported answer → bounded implication → concrete future check. Chinese closing conventions remain unobserved.

## Do

- End with what was demonstrated and what still needs testing; retain limits of observational traces, constructed baselines and repeated versions.

## Avoid

- No general-causality leap, new contribution, universal modern ad/session/persona default, fictional separate conclusion or author-persona imitation.
