## Rhetorical moves

- Common arc is aggregate context → RQ/hypothesis-specific comparison → figure/table evidence → interpretation → limits and practice. Old logs use session/query/term levels; advertising separates multiple KPI tests and practical significance; assisted-search distinguishes acceptance from performance; persona work separates coverage/stability from data validity.

## Organization

- Preserve descriptive, inferential, mechanistic and managerial roles. Some short papers merge results/discussion; magazine narratives lack independent conclusion. Shared versions elaborate one evidence stream, not additional replications.

## Language and stance

- Report measured local outcomes directly, hedge interpretations/transfer and retain unsupported/partial hypotheses. Terms such as effect or confirms in observational papers require a causality check; absence of significance is not equivalence.

## Evidence and citations

- Introduce what a visual compares, define denominator/comparator and interpret selected trends instead of reciting grids. Relative relevance, sponsored-click share, conversion rate/potential, revenue/profit/ROA and persona change/consistency are distinct. Large N can make trivial differences significant.

## Cross-lingual transferable patterns

- Transfer question → scoped evidence → interpretation → limitation. Chinese comparison wording and visual-discussion conventions have no corpus support.

## Do

- Keep source-specific units/support, disclose same-log/campaign/version overlap, identify different sample/window confounds and separate output plausibility from validated inputs.

## Avoid

- No copied user quotations, invented numerical precision, table-cell guesses, source-fact import, independent-evidence inflation or route-planning benchmark claims.

## Generation checklist

- Check question/design alignment, sample dependence, raw/transformed values, percentage versus percentage points, internal source inconsistencies and observed versus speculative implication.
