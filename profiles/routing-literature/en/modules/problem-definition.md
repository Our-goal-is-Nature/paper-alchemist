## Rhetorical moves

- Actual objectives/questions dominate this module, not formal optimization models. The expanded corpus decomposes usage into session/query/term or prevalence/behavior/effectiveness, distinguishes acceptance/timing/performance, maps theory-driven marketing KPIs to hypotheses and operationalizes user intent/segmentation criteria.

## Organization

- An objective paragraph can precede enumerated RQs or KPI hypotheses with justifications. Short genres state one question, journals expand conditions and definitions. No mathematical routing model, constraints or objective is present merely because a paper discusses travel or airline behavior.

## Language and stance

- Neutral interrogatives and explicit operational definitions establish a comparison before a result. A theoretical expectation or hypothesis is not a fact; unsupported hypotheses remain reportable outcomes.

## Evidence and citations

- Define measured constructs, proxies, category rules and comparators. IP/cookie session, keyword gender orientation, buying-funnel stage and attention-check validity are operational labels, not externally observed cognition/demographics/truth.
