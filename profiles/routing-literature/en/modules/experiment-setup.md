## Rhetorical moves

- Expanded setup evidence includes dated commercial logs, laboratory/TREC assistance and learning tasks, two-site sponsored-label experiments, social/API corpora, retailer campaigns and persona studies. Give setting/sample/access, inclusion and exclusions, protocol/condition or analysis window, and evaluation plan suited to the actual design.

## Organization

- Log studies need timestamps/windows/identifier limits; laboratory studies need participant/tasks, controlled differences, counterbalancing and instruments; observational marketing needs campaign/record and grouping definitions; longitudinal persona studies need collection failures and matching rules. Combined original sections remain combined.

## Language and stance

- Concrete collection/procedure reporting and source-specific justification dominate. Claims of representative/robust samples remain scoped; scale does not eliminate selection, measurement or dependence.
